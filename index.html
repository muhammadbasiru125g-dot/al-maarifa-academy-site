The Holy Qur'an — Full Reader

Read, review & memorise — all 114 surahs.

Study all 114 surahs with recitation, English translation, notes, starred ayat, favourites and memorisation tracking. Six short surahs are available as offline samples; other surahs and audio need an internet connection.

Surah & verse range
Online Arabic text (Uthmani) and the Muhammad Asad English translation are provided via AlQuran Cloud. A small set of short surahs is bundled as site samples; confirm translation attribution and usage rights before public redistribution. English is a translation, not the Arabic Qur’an. Verse-by-verse playback opens with the selected reciter’s basmala recording where applicable; Al-Fatihah already begins with it, and At-Tawbah (Surah 9) has no basmala. Salah Bukhatir’s full-surah audio is supplied via MP3Quran. Check source terms before republishing.
